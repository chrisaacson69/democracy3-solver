How to use one. Open the Bench, hit Reset to US start, then set each policy below to its target. Only policies that differ from the US start are listed — everything omitted stays where it starts. Ticks are remembered in this browser. The outcome figures are what the solver predicts; the Bench should reproduce them once every row is set.
Effects are measured by perturbing each policy from the US start (economy at its long-run average, crises free to move), re-solving the whole 1,149-edge network, and recording the change in all 40 outcomes — not by reading the policy’s own effect list. That distinction is not academic: Healthcare Vouchers has no edge to Health and still moves it +0.030, entirely through PrivateHealthcare. Per-dollar figures use the budget's grounded anchors; policies that improve an outcome and the balance are shown as free rather than given a meaningless ratio.
